QUESTION 3 - INSPECTION OF THE MED-AI PROJECT BOARD

Strengths of the Med-AI Project Board

1. Attractive and professional interface. The layout is clean and the colour scheme is well balanced, giving the impression of a finished product rather than a rough prototype.

2. Comes with all the utilities a project needs. It covers almost everything a team requires: an overview, a task board, a progress chart, member management, meeting scheduling and settings.

3. Easy to use without any instructions. Functions are placed where users expect to find them, so it is obvious what to click without having to learn the tool first.

4. Information is presented visually and is easy to grasp. Figures, colours and charts make it possible to understand the state of the project at a single glance.

5. Includes AI support. This is a modern touch that sets it apart from conventional project management tools.

6. Fully localised in Vietnamese. This suits domestic users and lowers the language barrier for team members.

7. Quick to access with nothing to install. Simply opening the link in a browser is enough to start using it, which makes sharing with the whole team very convenient.

8. Lightweight and stable. Pages load quickly and moving between sections feels smooth, with no sense of waiting.

9. Well suited to small teams and academic projects. It is not as heavy or complex as professional tools, yet offers just enough for real needs.

10. Highly customisable. Because it is developed in-house, features can be adjusted or added to match specific requirements.

Weakness of the Med-AI Project Board: The website has font rendering issues, and some features such as notifications are not functional.
